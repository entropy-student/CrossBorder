# COMP-001 Supplier Follow-up

Status: `OPEN`; component: `COMP-001`; bundle candidate:
`BUNDLE-PET-FUR-RESCUE-KIT`.

Ask the supplier for evidence or a direct answer to these missing facts. Do
not convert an answer into `VERIFIED` without the required evidence.

1. What is the supplier's official company name?
2. What are the full product dimensions, including depth?
3. What are the dimensions of one product in its OPP bag?
4. What is the weight of one packaged unit?
5. Which colors are currently available?
6. Do available colors have different pricing?
7. What are the supplier's variant or SKU IDs for each color?
8. What is the country of origin for the product?
9. Can the supplier ship from China directly to an individual US consumer?
10. If consumer dropshipping is supported, what price and shipping method
    apply?
